Lo que haceWhat it does

Eliges una carpeta de fotos, marcas cuáles entran y cambias de módulo sin volver a empezar. La configuración se guarda en la propia carpeta y se restaura al volver. Pick a folder of photos, check which ones are in, and switch modules without starting over. Settings are saved in the folder itself and restored when you come back.
